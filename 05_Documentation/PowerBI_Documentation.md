# Power BI Documentation

## Dashboard Name

Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

---

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 — Executive Overview

Purpose:
Provide an executive-level summary of customer churn.

KPIs:
- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Churn Rate
- Revenue at Risk %

Visuals:
- Churn Rate by Contract Type
- Churn Rate by Tenure Cohort
- Churn Rate by Internet Service
- Churn Rate by Payment Method

---

### Page 2 — Customer Drivers

Purpose:
Identify customer and service characteristics associated with churn.

Visuals:
- Churn Rate by Gender
- Churn Rate by Partner Status
- Churn Rate by Tech Support
- Churn Rate by Dependents
- Churn Rate by Senior Citizen Status
- Churn Rate by Online Security
- Churn Rate by Online Backup

A dedicated Key Business Insights section summarizes major patterns.

---

### Page 3 — Revenue & Contracts

Purpose:
Analyze the financial impact of customer churn.

KPIs:
- Total Monthly Revenue
- Churned Monthly Revenue
- Revenue at Risk %
- Average Monthly Charge

Visuals:
- Total Monthly Revenue by Contract Type
- Churned Monthly Revenue by Contract Type
- Customer Distribution by Monthly Charges
- Churned Monthly Revenue by Tenure Cohort

Filters:
- Gender
- Contract
- Internet Service
- Tenure Cohort

A Reset Filters button is provided for interactive analysis.

---

### Page 4 — Risk & Retention

Purpose:
Identify customer risk segments and support retention analysis.

KPIs:
- Active Customers
- High Risk Customers
- High Risk %
- High Risk Monthly Revenue

Visuals:
- Customer Distribution by Risk Category
- Active Customers by Risk Score
- Customer risk detail table

Risk categories:
- Low Risk
- Medium Risk
- High Risk

---

## Navigation

Page navigation is implemented using Power BI navigation buttons.

Users can move between:

Executive Overview → Customer Drivers → Revenue & Contracts → Risk & Retention

---

## Tooltip

A dedicated tooltip page named:

`TT_Churn_Segment`

is used to provide additional contextual information when users hover over applicable visuals.

---

## Smart Narrative

Smart Narrative has been incorporated to provide dynamic contextual information for dashboard visuals.

---

## Reset Filters

A Reset Filters button is implemented on the Revenue & Contracts page.

The button restores the default filter state.

---

## Dashboard Design

The dashboard uses:

- Consistent typography
- Consistent spacing
- Blue primary visual theme
- KPI cards
- Rounded visual containers
- Interactive navigation
- Business insight sections
- Minimal visual clutter
