TELECOM CUSTOMER CHURN — BUSINESS INSIGHTS

Purpose
-------
This file contains the concise business findings generated from the telecom churn analysis.
Use the actual values generated by the Python output and Power BI dashboard when presenting
the project. Do not treat observed relationships as proof of causation.

Recommended insight sections
----------------------------
1. Executive Overview
   - Total customers
   - Churned customers
   - Overall churn rate
   - Retained customers / retention rate
   - Observed monthly revenue
   - Churned monthly revenue
   - Revenue at risk

2. Contract
   - Compare churn rate across contract types.
   - Identify the segment with the highest observed churn rate.
   - Use the finding as a retention-analysis signal, not a causal conclusion.

3. Tenure
   - Compare churn across tenure cohorts.
   - Highlight the cohort with the highest observed churn.
   - Use the result to identify where early-tenure retention analysis may be useful.

4. Internet Service
   - Compare churn rates across internet-service categories.
   - Highlight meaningful differences visible in the segment table/dashboard.

5. Payment Method
   - Compare churn rates by payment method.
   - Highlight the highest observed churn segment.

6. Customer Experience
   - Review tech-support, online-security and online-backup segments.
   - Identify observed churn differences that may warrant deeper investigation.

7. Customer Risk
   - Summarize the SQL rule-based risk categories.
   - Report active high-risk customers and associated monthly revenue exposure when available.
   - Clearly distinguish the SQL business-rule score from the Python ML model.

8. Machine Learning
   - Report the model-comparison metrics generated by Python.
   - Include Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 and ROC-AUC.
   - Explain that test-set metrics describe model performance on the evaluated dataset.

Business interpretation
-----------------------
The project is designed to support questions about customer retention, churn drivers,
revenue exposure and customer-risk segmentation.

Important limitations
---------------------
- Segment comparisons are descriptive.
- Association does not establish causation.
- The SQL risk score is a rule-based business framework and is not the same as an ML prediction.
- Revenue-at-risk calculations use the project's defined observed revenue methodology.
- ML performance depends on the dataset, preprocessing, split and model configuration.
